About BiswaPath

BiswaPath (বিশ্বপাঠ) is a free, independent education website for Bengali-speaking students in West Bengal, Bangladesh and around the world. It covers three stages of a student's journey: school, studying abroad, and research and PhD careers.

Our promises

  • Free for students, permanently. No subscriptions, paid courses or premium content.
  • Checked by people. Every article is reviewed by a person before it is published, including anything drafted with AI tools.
  • Official sources first. Visa, fee and scholarship information always links to the official page.
  • Your data is not for sale.

Founder

BiswaPath was founded in 2026 by Dr Sumit Konar, a chemist with a PhD and international research experience, based in the UK. He also runs Global Puja Parikrama, a map of Durga Pujas around the world.

How it is built

The website is built with the help of AI tools (Claude and ChatGPT) and reviewed by people. Content is written or checked by named contributors.

A note on advice

BiswaPath gives general information, not personal advice. For visa or immigration questions, always use the official government website or a regulated adviser.
