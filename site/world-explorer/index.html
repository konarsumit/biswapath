World Explorer | বিশ্বপাঠ বিশ্বমানচিত্র

One map for two questions every student asks before going abroad: where could I study? and who will be near me when I get there?

Open the map in Google Maps Opens in a new tab; on a phone it can open in the Google Maps app.

The two layers

  • Universities — Universities listed in our University finder. Fees, deadlines and scholarships are on the finder, with links to official pages.
  • Bengali Communities — Bengali associations and Durga Puja organisers, from the Global Puja Parikrama directory. They are a starting point for finding people near you, all year round.

How to use it

  1. Click the list icon at the top left of the map to show or hide each layer.
  2. Click a marker to see its name and details.
  3. Zoom in on the city you are considering to see both layers together.

Please note

  • Community markers may show an approximate city location, not the exact venue.
  • Puja entries are historical records from past years. They are not confirmed current events — check dates and venues with the organisers before you travel.
  • Always confirm university information on the university's own website.

World Explorer is free to use, like everything on BiswaPath. The map is hosted by Google My Maps.
