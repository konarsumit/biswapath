Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version

You can read everything on BiswaPath without an account. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share your data.

What we collect

  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Forms. If you fill in our contributor form (run on Google Forms), we receive the details you choose to give, such as your name, email and subject. We use them only to contact you about contributing, and delete them if you ask.

Young people

Many of our readers are school students. We do not ask students under 18 for personal information, and the site has no accounts, comments or messaging.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete information you have sent us. Contact details will be published here before launch.

Who is responsible: Dr Sumit Konar, United Kingdom.
