Become a contributor

BiswaPath grows through people who explain things well. You do not need to write a lot: one clear article on a topic you know can help thousands of students.

Who we are looking for

  • Teachers of chemistry, physics, biology and mathematics
  • Researchers and PhD students in any subject
  • Bengali students studying abroad who can share their experience
  • People who can translate carefully between Bengali and English

How it works

  1. Fill in the short form below with your subject and background.
  2. We agree a topic with you.
  3. You write, we review and check facts, and you approve the final version.
  4. The article is published with your name.

Contributing is voluntary and unpaid. You keep the right to be named on everything you write.
