বিশ্বপাঠBiswaPath

পাঠ means a lesson. পথ means a path. In English letters both are written “path”, and BiswaPath means both: learning from the world, and a path into it.

Free learning and honest guidance for Bengali-speaking students in West Bengal, Bangladesh and around the world, from school chemistry to a funded PhD.
