Editorial policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

  • Accuracy first. Every scientific and educational article is fact-checked before publication.
  • Human review of AI drafts. AI tools may help draft or translate, but nothing is published without a person reviewing it.
  • Official sources. University, scholarship, fee and visa information links to the official source and shows the date it was checked.
  • Dates on every article. Each article shows when it was published and last updated.
  • Named authors. Contributors are named, and their stated background is checked.
  • Copyright. We do not copy exam papers or other people's teaching materials. We link to them.
  • Natural Bengali. Translations are reviewed by a Bengali speaker for accuracy and readability.
  • Corrections. If you find a mistake, tell us and we will fix it and note the change.
