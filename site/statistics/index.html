Students abroad: the numbers

How many students from India and Bangladesh study abroad, where they go, and what they study. Countries count students in different ways — visas issued, students enrolled, or people who say “study” at the airport — so numbers from different sources are not directly comparable.

India

About 18.82 lakh Indian students were studying abroad in 2025, in 153 countries. The five largest destinations: [1]

For medicine abroad, check the National Medical Commission's rules before you choose: they decide whether a foreign MBBS lets you practise in India.

Which subjects?

  • Germany (Indian students): engineering 60%, social sciences, economics and management 21%, maths and natural sciences 18% [7].
  • USA: 57% of all international students study STEM subjects [4]. The last published split for Indian students (2018/19) showed nearly 70% in maths, computer science or engineering [5].
  • UK (2023/24): nearly one in four Indian students chose computing and IT or health and medicine; 12% of Bangladeshi students chose computing and IT, up from 9% in 2019/20. Business and management is still the largest subject overall [12].

What nobody counts

Bengali students. India's government reports only national totals — a reply to Parliament asked for state-wise figures and gave none [21] — and no country records students by mother tongue. Bangladesh's figures are, in practice, almost all Bengali students; for West Bengal there is no published number.

Subject by university. No official source shows which universities, say, Indian chemistry students choose compared with economics students. For that, use subject rankings and the course pages in our University finder.

Help us count. If you are a Bengali student abroad, tell us your subject and university (and, if you like, your home district) through the Contribute page. With enough answers, BiswaPath will publish the first map of where Bengali students study, by subject — counts only, never names.
